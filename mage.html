⚔️ Rotations ❄ MAGE

What to press in each situation, using your mage keys. Spells with a level next to them unlock later. Until then, skip that step.

🎯 One normal mob your bread and butter

  1. 1 Frostbolt from max range to pull
  2. 1 Frostbolt again while it walks to you slowed
  3. If it gets close: Q Frost Nova, step back, R Ice Lance (big damage on frozen targets)
  4. Finish with 2 Fire Blast, or 4 wand if you're low on mana

💡 When Fingers of Frost procs, your next R Ice Lance hits like the target is frozen. Spend it right away.

👥 Two or three mobs accidental pulls

  1. Humanoid or beast? E Polymorph one first
  2. Q Frost Nova the rest, F Blink back
  3. 6 Cone of Cold 26 when they bunch up, then Frostbolt one down
  4. X Ice Barrier 40 before the pull, Shift+G Mana Shield if they reach you

💡 Undead and elementals can't be sheeped. Nova + kite instead.

🌨️ Big pulls AoE packs once you're 40+

  1. Round up a pack, Q Frost Nova, back out of range
  2. Shift+Q Blizzard on them, then 6 Cone of Cold when they break out
  3. Shift+X Cold Snap to get a 2nd Frost Nova + Cone of Cold
  4. Going wrong? Z Ice Block, then Blink away

💡 Blizzard got nerfed in Forever. It still works, but mobs don't stay slowed as long, so keep your Nova ready.

💪 Elite or tough mob quest bosses, rares

  1. Buff up: Arcane Intellect + Frost/Ice Armor, full mana, mana gem ready
  2. 1 Frostbolt, Frostbolt as it runs in
  3. Q Frost Nova, F Blink away, Frostbolt again. Repeat
  4. Shift+X Cold Snap ~28 for a second Nova round
  5. Low mana: Shift+4 mana gem, then Shift+T Evocation when it's safe

🗡️ Rogue opens on you stunned out of nowhere

  1. Stun ends: Q Frost Nova right away, then F Blink out
  2. E Polymorph them, eat/drink a sip or reposition
  3. 1 Frostbolt, then R Ice Lance while they're frozen (Shatter)
  4. Stuck in a root? Shift+F Escape Artist (gnome racial)
  5. Low? Z Ice Block ~30 to wait out their cooldowns

🪓 Warrior / melee keep them off you

  1. 1 Frostbolt as they come in, they're slowed
  2. Q Frost Nova when they reach you, Blink, Frostbolt
  3. 6 Cone of Cold to keep them slowed, R Ice Lance on the frozen ones
  4. Shift+X Cold Snap for a 2nd Nova. X Ice Barrier 40 soaks their burst

🔥 Casters mage, warlock, priest

  1. T Counterspell their big cast (Fireball, Shadow Bolt, Heal). It locks that school
  2. E Polymorph healers or the 2nd enemy
  3. 1 Frostbolt + 2 Fire Blast to burst. Use a Spellblasting Potion for even more
  4. Free Action Potion only stops stuns + slows, not fears or sheeps, so save it for melee fights

🏹 Hunter annoying at range

  1. E Polymorph the pet or Q Nova it
  2. Use cover (trees, rocks) to break line of sight, then make them come to you
  3. Q Frost Nova + R Ice Lance, 2 Fire Blast

🏃 Ganked by someone way higher don't fight, survive

  1. Q Frost Nova, F Blink, run toward guards
  2. E Polymorph them if they're alone
  3. Swiftness Potion or Lesser Invisibility Potion from the Potions tab
  4. Last resort: Z Ice Block and hope a friend shows up. Or Teleport home

⚔️ DPS (your job)

  1. Before the pull: Arcane Intellect on every caster, armor on you. Conjure water Shift+E and hand some to the healer
  2. Crowd control: moon 🌙 marker = your sheep. E Polymorph before the pull and leave it alone
  3. Single target: wait for 2-3 tank hits, then 1 Frostbolt spam. R Ice Lance on Fingers of Frost procs
  4. Big packs: only once the tank has everything: Q Nova, Shift+Q Blizzard / G Arcane Explosion, 6 Cone of Cold

🚨 When things go wrong

  1. Mob on you: stop casting, Q Nova, F Blink toward the tank
  2. Still on you: X Ice Barrier / Shift+G Mana Shield, then Z Ice Block
  3. Curse on someone: Shift+3 Remove Lesser Curse
  4. Caster healing itself: T Counterspell
Mana tip: use your mana gem first, then Evocation Shift+T on bosses so the group doesn't wait on you.

🔌 Addon + sync

The ForeverGuide addon reads your level, XP, zone, quest log, turned-in quests, gear, hearth, bag space and AH prices (from Auctioneer, Auctionator or TSM), then gives you a code to paste here.

PASTE YOUR CODE in game: type /fg, then Ctrl+C
INSTALL THE ADDON one time

⬇ Download ForeverGuide.zip

1. Unzip

Inside is a folder called ForeverGuide.

2. Drop it in

Put that folder in your WoW Forever Interface\AddOns folder. In the Battle.net app, hit the gear next to Play → Show in Explorer, then open the Forever game folder → Interface → AddOns. Make the folders if they're missing.

3. Turn it on

At character select, click AddOns and make sure ForeverGuide is ticked. If it says out of date, tick Load out of date AddOns.

4. Sync

In game, type /fg. A box pops up with your code already selected. Press Ctrl+C, come here, paste it above, hit Sync.

When to sync

Whenever you want a fresh "right now" list: after a batch of turn-ins, at an inn, after you level. Takes 5 seconds.

Heads up: the addon can only remember quests you turn in after you install it. Quests you did before that won't show as synced, so check those off by hand once and you're set.

🧪 PvP potions + alchemy gold

Your alchemy: 150 (updates when you sync). Prices come from your AH addon when you sync, and you can type over any of them. Profit already takes off the 5% AH cut.

Free Action Potion is the money maker. Every PvP player wants one, and on PvP realms they sell fast (hundreds a day on the big US PvP realms). Buy the recipe from Soolie Berryfizz in Ironforge. She has limited stock, so if she's sold out, check back later or look on the AH. The hard part is Blackmouth Oil: fish Oily Blackmouth off the Menethil coast while you're in the Wetlands.
YOUR AH PRICES type like 45s, 1g 20s, or just 45 for silver · saved on this device
Merchant's Favor: the new Forever recipes (Spellblasting, Frenzy, the Smog throwables) come from Nina Surefire at Three Corners in Redridge. You pay in Merchant's Favor, which you earn by turning in filled supply crates at the trading camps. You're already in Redridge on this route, so stock up on it there.
Selling tips: list in small stacks (FAP stacks to 5). Post on Thursday-Saturday evenings when people raid and PvP. Don't undercut by half, just a few copper. If the AH is flooded, hold your stock for a few days. Healing potions moved to First Aid in Forever, so alchemy doesn't make them anymore.
