⚔️ Dungeon dps (Lv 22-26) ❄ MAGE

The priest heals in these, you're DPS. Your job: big damage, never pull aggro, and lock down extra mobs.

Before the pull

Arcane Intellect Shift+1 on every caster, Frost Armor Shift+2 on you. Conjure water Shift+E and hand some to the healer. They'll love you.

Crowd control

If the tank marks a mob with a moon 🌙, that's your sheep: Polymorph E before the pull, then leave it alone. Stockade mobs are all humanoids, so they can all be sheeped.

Single target

Wait for 2-3 tank hits, then Frostbolt 1 spam. Ice Lance R and Fire Blast 2 while moving.

Big packs

Only once the tank has every mob: Frost Nova Q, then Arcane Explosion G spam. Cone of Cold 6 at 26. Too early = everything runs at you.

Aggro on you

Stop casting, Frost Nova Q, Blink F toward the tank. Mana Shield Shift+G if it's still hitting you.

Utility

Remove Lesser Curse for curses. Counterspell T (24) on Defias casters' big heals.

Mana tip: Evocation Shift+T refills your mana in 8 sec. Save it for boss fights so the group doesn't have to wait on you.

🧪 PvP potions + alchemy gold

Your alchemy: 150 (updates when you sync). Prices swing a lot between realms, so type in what your AH shows and it does the math. Profit already takes off the 5% AH cut.

Free Action Potion is the money maker. Every PvP player wants one, and on PvP realms they sell fast (hundreds a day on the big US PvP realms). Buy the recipe from Soolie Berryfizz in Ironforge. She has limited stock, so if she's sold out, check back later or look on the AH. The hard part is Blackmouth Oil: fish Oily Blackmouth off the Menethil coast while you're in the Wetlands.

Merchant's Favor: the new Forever recipes (Spellblasting, Frenzy, the Smog throwables) come from Nina Surefire at Three Corners in Redridge. You pay in Merchant's Favor, which you earn by turning in filled supply crates at the trading camps. You're already in Redridge on this route, so stock up on it there.
Selling tips: list in small stacks (FAP stacks to 5). Post on Thursday-Saturday evenings when people raid and PvP. Don't undercut by half, just a few copper. If the AH is flooded, hold your stock for a few days. Healing potions moved to First Aid in Forever, so alchemy doesn't make them anymore.

The ForeverGuide addon reads your level, XP, zone, quest log, turned-in quests, gear, hearth, talent points and bag space, then gives you a code to paste here.

⬇ Download ForeverGuide.zip

1. Unzip

Inside is a folder called ForeverGuide.

2. Drop it in

Put that folder in your WoW Forever Interface\AddOns folder. In the Battle.net app, hit the gear next to Play → Show in Explorer, then open the Forever game folder → Interface → AddOns. Make the folders if they're missing.

3. Turn it on

At character select, click AddOns and make sure ForeverGuide is ticked. If it says out of date, tick Load out of date AddOns.

4. Sync

In game, type /fg. A box pops up with your code already selected. Press Ctrl+C, come here, paste it above, hit Sync.

When to sync

Whenever you want a fresh "right now" list: after a batch of turn-ins, at an inn, after you level. Takes 5 seconds.

Heads up: the addon can only remember quests you turn in after you install it. Quests you did before that won't show as synced, so check those off by hand once and you're set.
